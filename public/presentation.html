✅ Multidimensional Feasibility
  • Technical Viability: Built on open-source, production-grade web stack (React 19, Supabase); zero proprietary hardware required.
  • Data Feasibility: Integrates directly with existing govt databases — PM-KISAN, Aadhaar, State Land Records & official CACP MSP rates.
  • Zero-Bar Adoption: Multilingual AI Sahayak (Hindi/Hinglish voice) eliminates digital literacy barriers for marginal farmers.
  • Infrastructure Cost: Cloud-native setup; mandis require only existing electronic weighbridges & standard smartphones/tablets.
🛡️ Challenges & Mitigation Strategies
  • Rural Network Latency: PWA offline queue caching + automated SMS slot confirmation fallbacks.
  • Operator Workflow Friction: Intuitive Centre Console reduces 60% paperwork with 1-click digital J-Form invoices.
  • Weighment Tampering Risk: Direct digital gross/tare capture with automated immutable audit logging.
  • Scale to 7,000+ Mandis: Supabase RLS district partitioning + Vercel Edge CDN for high-throughput scaling.
